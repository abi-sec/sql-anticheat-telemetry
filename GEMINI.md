# Writing Style & Tone

When writing notes, findings, or any documentation in this project:
- **Do not use em dashes (`—`) anywhere.** Use hyphens (`-`) or rewrite the sentence.
- **Match the user's tone:** Keep it casual, concise, and direct (e.g., "crazy high kill averages", "textbook burner account", "popped up a lot").
- Avoid overly formal phrasing.
